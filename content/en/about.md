# About ReFi Provence

## Our Mission

ReFi Provence connects Southern France's regenerative movements with global innovations in finance, technology, and governance to accelerate the transition toward a life-centered economy.

## Our Story

ReFi Provence is a local node of ReFi DAO, a global organization that promotes and facilitates the development of the ReFi ecosystem.

## Our Values

- **Regeneration**: We believe in a holistic approach that regenerates both social and ecological systems
- **Collaboration**: We build bridges between local and global movements
- **Innovation**: We explore how technology can serve nature and communities
- **Participatory Governance**: We foster collective and democratic decision-making processes

## Our Team

ReFi Provence is a collective of practitioners working to create a collaborative, self-governed society.
