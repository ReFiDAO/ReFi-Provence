# Join the Community

We're building the connections that make regeneration possible, but we can't do it alone!

## How to Participate

### As a Local Project

If you're working on a regenerative project in Southern France, we'd love to know about it. Join our events or [contact us](/en/contact) directly.

### As a Funder

We connect funders with local regenerative projects that need support. [Contact us](/en/contact) to learn more.

### As a Community Member

Join our events and workshops to meet others passionate about regeneration and regenerative finance.

### As a Partner

We're always looking for new partners to collaborate on regenerative initiatives. [Learn how to support us](/en/how-to-support-us).

## Next Steps

- [Contact us](/en/contact) to discuss your participation
- Join our [events](/en/events)
- [Learn how to support us](/en/how-to-support-us)
