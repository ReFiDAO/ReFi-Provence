# How to Support Us

There are several ways to support ReFi Provence in our mission to connect local regenerative movements with the global ReFi ecosystem.

## Donations

Donations help us organize events, develop projects, and build our community infrastructure.

To make a donation, [contact us](/en/contact) for payment information.

## Partnerships

We're looking for partners to collaborate on regenerative initiatives. If your organization shares our values and wants to collaborate, [contact us](/en/contact).

## Event Participation

Attending our events and workshops is a great way to support our community and meet others passionate about regeneration.

## Knowledge Sharing

If you have expertise in regenerative finance, technology, governance, or other relevant areas, we'd love to share your knowledge with our community.

## Volunteering

We always need volunteers to help organize events, create content, and grow our community.

## Next Steps

- [Contact us](/en/contact) to discuss the best way to support us
- Join our [events](/en/events)
- [Learn about our partners](/en/partners)
